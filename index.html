Privacy

Made with love - zero snooping 💕

To make the game better we count, completely anonymously, how it is played:

  • when the game is opened
  • which level starts, is won, is lost or is left
  • how many swipes a level took, and whether the hint bulb helped
  • when a result is shared

That is everything. Each count stands alone - nothing links the counts to you or to each other.

No cookies. No tracking pixels, no fingerprinting, no ads. We store no names, no e-mail addresses, no device IDs and no IP addresses. Nothing is ever sold or shared with anyone.

Like every website, our host (Cloudflare) briefly processes your IP address to deliver the page itself; it is not stored with the play counters.

Because no personal data is collected or stored, no consent banner is required under EU rules (GDPR / ePrivacy).
